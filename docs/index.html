Verba Volant

One Latin saying every morning, on Telegram.

Vae victis

wy WIK-tis

“Woe to the vanquished.”

According to Livy, the Gallic chief Brennus said it after sacking Rome, throwing his sword on the scale as the ransom was weighed. The winner writes the rules.
