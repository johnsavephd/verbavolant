One Latin saying every morning, on Telegram.

Vae victis

wy WIK-tis

“Woe to the vanquished.”

Livy, book 5: after sacking Rome around 390 BC, the Gallic chief Brennus used false weights for the ransom, and when the Romans protested he threw his sword on the scale.

The winner sets the terms. Said whenever the stronger side stops pretending to be fair.
